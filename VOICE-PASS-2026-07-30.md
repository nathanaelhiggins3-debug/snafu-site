## Voice rules applied

Read this before scanning the rewrites so you can spot the ones I got wrong.

- Direct, punchy sentences. Body copy in full sentences, not fragments.
- No contractions in prose (it is / that is / do not / cannot / will not / would not).
- No swearing.
- No hypebeast tells: no urgency, no FOMO, no ALL CAPS shouting inside sentences, no "cop before it goes," no "move quick."
- No banned register: "creative director" and adjacent new-age fashion terms. "Curated" flagged for review — you did not explicitly ban it, but it sits in the same drawer.
- No AI-default cadence: parallel two-fragment patterns like "Same hand. Different math." or "Not X. Not Y. Just Z."
- Headings, buttons, meta labels, decorative footer taglines: fragments and short forms allowed by nature. I did not flag those unless they broke another rule (contraction, hype, banned word).
- Meaning and facts preserved. Only register, rhythm, and word choice changed.

## Summary of patterns caught

Ranked by how much they hurt the site's voice, most first.

1. **The "Not X. Not Y. Just Z." fragment stack.** Appeared in About hero, Shop curation, Deals curation, Goods sub, Play lede, Deals lede. This is the single loudest AI-default cadence on the site. Every instance rewritten to a full sentence that says the same thing.
2. **Contractions.** Across almost every page. Roughly 30 instances flagged. Worn In alone has about a dozen.
3. **"Curated" / "curated."** Three uses (Shop lede, About hero, About section title). Flagged for your call — you may want to allow it, but your calibration puts it in the suspect drawer.
4. **Body-copy fragments used for rhythm** (About: "Nothing else does." / "And so on." / "Ready to wear on arrival." — Product footer: "Every item cleaned. Every item authenticated." — Field Notes teaser opener). Rewritten as sentences.
5. **One hypebeast tell.** Cart empty state: "so move quick." Cut. This is the only line on the site that reads like the anti-reference you named.
6. **One rhetorical question** in the Goods footer. Replaced with a plain statement.

## What I did not touch

- Headings and titles (Dispatches, Editorial, Field Notes, Interview, Gallery, Shop, Deals, Play, Goods, About, Worn In).
- Nav links, back buttons, section names, buttons ("BUY NOW," "ADD TO PACK," "PROCEED TO CHECKOUT").
- Scene / decorative labels ("Back to the clearing," "Choose your desk," "Read on," "Descend," "Enter," "\ end of the path \," "\ deepest point \").
- Colophon strings and footer taglines that read as brand mark (except where they broke a rule).
- The `.snafu-line` note pattern — no content in the current data files to review.
- All product descriptions in `js/shop-data.js` and `js/goods-data.js`. Those are data files with per-piece copy, and worth a separate pass. Let me know if you want that.
