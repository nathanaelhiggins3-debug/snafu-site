## Non-negotiable release photo set

Each Shop piece needs, where applicable:

1. Full front
2. Full back
3. Label / tag
4. Construction or signature detail
5. Any flaw or wear called out in condition

An item can be beautifully photographed and still fail this test. The buyer
needs enough proof to trust a one-of-one object without DMing for basics.

## Completion definition

A product is release-ready only when its page gives a buyer enough information
to make a decision, and its photos make SNAFU look more considered than a
marketplace listing. Check its data, photos, price, category, and availability,
then mark it ready for the chosen launch set.
