About · Los Angeles

SNAFU is a universe built around culture, clothing and community. Stories first. Everything else grows from there.

The outlet

News, shows, interviews and the things worth paying attention to. Written and shot by hand, not pulled from a feed.

The community

SNAFU is shaped by the people in it. Read, save what hits, and say what you think in Field Notes. The conversation is half the point.

The collection

Alongside the stories sits a small collection of pieces. Curated, sourced and hand-picked. Every one is cleaned and ready to wear. When it's gone, it's gone.

The world

Everything here lives in a 16-bit world. The people, the stories and the clothes are real. The pixels are just how we show it.
