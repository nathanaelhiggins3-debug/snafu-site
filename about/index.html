Statement · Est. 2026

SNAFU is a curated archive of vintage garments picked one at a time. Not an algorithm. Not a resale marketplace. A point of view.

How it's curated

Every piece was pulled by hand — from archives, estates, thrift bins, private collections. Inspected in person. Bought because it deserved to be seen again.

A $20 find gets the same set of eyes as a $500 archive piece. The price sorts them into tiers. Nothing else does.

Authentication

Every item passes a SNAFU authentication check specific to its category. Jackets: original tags, period-correct hardware, lining construction. Tees: stitch construction, tag stock, print method, wear pattern. Denim: selvedge presence, rivet marking, wash consistency with era. And so on.

The check is documented per item and shown on its page. The badge is not decoration.

Cleaning

Every item is thoroughly cleaned before it enters inventory. Ready to wear on arrival. No exceptions for tier.

Two tiers, one standard

SNAFU SHOP is the mainline — rarer, older, higher price. SNAFU DEALS is the second tier — more accessible pieces at real deal prices, mostly bin finds. Both are picked the same way. Deal items carry a different-colored border so you can tell them apart at a glance, whether they show up on their own page or mixed into the main shop grid.

The pixel world

The garments are real. The presentation is 16-bit — a world built to hold them. Every product has a cover generated at 256×384 pixels. The forest is where you enter. The shop is a valley over the ridge. The deals are the trading post beyond it.

What you're buying is real cotton, real wool, real silk. What you're looking at is SNAFU's way of showing it to you.
